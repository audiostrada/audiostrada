# Code of conduct

AudioStrada is made by and for people who make music, whatever their level, gear or genre.

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers: everyone started with a first recording.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexual content, in any space of the project.
- Help others find their answer rather than giving orders.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block people who do not respect
these rules. Report a problem to contact@audiostrada.com; reports stay confidential.

These rules follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
