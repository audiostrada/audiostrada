# Built-in cabinet impulse responses

The simulated cabinets play impulse responses from Guitarix (gx_cabinet_data.cc,
https://github.com/brummer10/guitarix), copyright Hermann Meyer, James Warden, Andreas Degert and
Pete Shorthose, licensed under the GNU GPL v2 or later. Stored as 48 kHz little-endian f32.
