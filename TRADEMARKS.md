You may, without asking:

- build AudioStrada from its source and use it, under its name, for yourself or your organisation;
- package an unmodified AudioStrada for a Linux distribution or a package manager under its name,

- talk about AudioStrada, write about it, review it, teach it, and use its name and logo for that.

- distribute a modified AudioStrada (beyond packaging fixes), a fork, or a build with features

  ones.

When in doubt, open an issue: we are glad to help.
